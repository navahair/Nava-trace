Don't take our word for it

A record is only worth something if you can check it. Run these on the bundle in your hand and see whether it matches what we claimed above.

  1. Bleach test. Take 10 strands, lift them. Raw hair goes light and stays elastic. Processed hair turns to gum.
  2. Water test. Wet it, let it dry with no product. Raw hair dries clean. Coated hair tangles the moment the silicone rinses off.
  3. Cuticle test. Run two fingers up the strand. It should feel rough one way and smooth the other. Smooth both ways means the cuticle was stripped.
  4. Burn test. Real hair smells like burnt keratin and turns to grey ash. Synthetic melts into a hard bead and smells like plastic.
  5. Weigh it. 100g dry. If it weighs light, you were charged for air.

If any test fails on this batch ID, message us with the batch number. We will replace it.
